Account

You do not need an account to play. An account lets you tie your in-game player key to your name, and it is how people get permission to upload old demos or host a server.

Usernames are 3 to 24 letters, digits, _ or -. Passwords are at least 10 characters. We send one mail to confirm the address.

Demo uploads

Uploading old client demos needs permission. If you have an old demo upload key, enter it once to upgrade this account: it gets upload permission and takes over what that key uploaded. The key is not used for anything else. Otherwise ask an admin.

Your servers

Public servers that anyone can join can be listed here. A server must be reachable from the internet over UDP (the game port) and over a secure WebSocket (wss:// with a valid certificate), so browsers can join too. An admin approves each one.

Put this in the server's configuration (it is shown once and works for one registration):

Also set AGENT_REGISTRATION_URL (this site), AGENT_PUBLIC_NAMES (the public game address as host:port) and AGENT_PUBLIC_URL (the https address of the agent), then start the server. It shows up here once the site has reached it.

Your player keys

Your game makes a player key by itself, and the stats you play are kept under it. While you are signed in here, playing in the browser links the key to this account automatically (once for each browser, on any server). The launcher does the same: press Sign in at its top right and approve it on this site. It happens in your browser: the key never leaves it, only a signature that proves it is yours. Your matches and stats then show up under your account name.

View your player page →

Each key is shown with its whole player id, the same one the game prints with /s4nd_id. Site admins only ever see the first 10 characters of an id, never the whole thing (in their lists and in the log).

Playing the original RTCW client, without the launcher or the browser?

Link its key by hand: get a code here, type /link CODE in game (with the slash), then confirm the key below. It only works on servers that offer it.

Email address

We send a link to the new address. Your account keeps its current address until you open it, and a note goes to the old one when it changes. Use this to fix a typo or if you lost access to the old mailbox.

Delete account

Removes the account, its permissions and its links, and erases your game stats: your name and keys become "Deleted Player" in every game you played, and your totals and player page are removed. This cannot be undone and the stats cannot be recovered. Other players' results stay, and so do demos you uploaded.
