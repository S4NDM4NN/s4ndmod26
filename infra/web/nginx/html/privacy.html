Privacy

Last updated: November 14, 2026. This describes what S4NDMoD26 actually stores and does today, and it will change as the project does.

Who we are

S4NDMoD26 is a hobby project that modernizes Return to Castle Wolfenstein multiplayer: browser and desktop play, player statistics, match history and replays. It is run from the United States. For anything below, write to s4ndm4nn@s4ndmod.com.

What is public

Statistics, match histories, replays and player pages are public, including for players with no account, shown under the name used in the game. The stats page lists the players who played most recently. If a key is linked to an account, its player page shows the account's username and picture and adds up all of that account's keys. Email addresses and the private key names are never public.

Linking keys to an account

You can link the keys your browser, the launcher and the original game client use to one account, so your games appear together. Linking is done by proving you hold the key. You can remove a link on your account page at any time.

Erasing your data

With an account, delete it. "Delete account" on your account page (it asks for your password) removes your account and also erases your game stats: your games show "Deleted Player" instead of your name and key, and your totals and player page are removed. This cannot be undone, and we cannot recover the stats afterwards. Unlinking a key without deleting the account keeps its stats.

Without an account, prove the key is yours by linking it: create an account, link your key (the launcher and the browser game do this for you when you are signed in; the original client can use /link), then delete the account. Linking needs your game to sign with the key, which is what proves it is yours. If that does not work for you, email us and we will help; we will not erase a key on an email alone.

Other players' kills, deaths and results stay, since they are theirs too. Replays are not rewritten: they expire by themselves. The game servers' own statistics files still hold their old lines, so we keep a one-way hash of an erased key to stop the profile being rebuilt from them.

Community servers

Independent servers may report statistics to this site in the future. Their operators keep their own recordings and logs under their own practices; this page covers what runs on s4ndmod.com.

Security

Passwords are hashed, sessions and tokens are stored hashed, and players prove their identity with signatures rather than passwords. No system is perfectly secure.

Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have the right to see, correct, export or delete personal data and to object to its use. Deleting your account erases your game stats too; for anything else, write to us and we will do what applies. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Changes

We update this page when what we do changes, and the date at the top says when.
